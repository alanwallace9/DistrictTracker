My Recommendation

Theme 1 (Trustworthy Blue) is my recommendation because:

  • Aligns with your existing bolt.new mockup - continuity matters
  • Blue conveys trust and reliability - exactly what "confident data" needs
  • Professional/institutional feel matches school district buyers
  • Most familiar to EdTech users (Skyward, PowerSchool, Focus all use blue)
  • Excellent accessibility with clear status color differentiation

However, Theme 2 (Modern Slate) is a close second if you want a more premium, contemporary feel.
